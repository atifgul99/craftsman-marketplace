
# Individual Return Preparation (Form 1040)

The return-prep **control**. It owns the source→workpaper→line contract, the
limitation ordering, the MAGI register, the basis-ownership map, the compliance
gates, and the completion conclusion.

It owns no doctrine. Arithmetic belongs to `estimate.md` (through
`close-estimate.md`); every substantive rule belongs to the module named in the
right-hand column.

| Question | Owner |
|---|---|
| What is the tax? | `close-estimate.md` → `estimate.md` |
| What must I pay and when? | `close-estimate.md` → `quarterly.md` |
| Is a doctrine applied correctly? | the domain module |
| Is the return complete, timely, and internally consistent? | **this file** |

Authorizes workpaper writes only within the granted scope; never payment.

**The authority seam.** `authority.md` governs every exact value — and also every
**outcome-determinative proposition**: eligibility, characterization, ordering,
release, deadline, transition rule, or method. A qualitative rule in this skill
is a prompt to verify, not a substitute for verifying. Background doctrine the
model already knows is not documented here and does not create a hold.

**Form line numbers are illustrative.** The 1040 line map changed across the
supported years (Schedule 1-A and 13a/13b exist only where OBBBA applies).
Resolve the target-year contract from the form before asserting a line-based
invariant — this file is routinely used for prior-year and amended work.

## 1. Preflight

**Step 0 — deadlines and standing.** Original and extended due dates
(`calendar.md`); whether Form 4868 was filed and accepted; **an extension to file
is not an extension to pay** (§6651(a)(2) runs regardless, and a non-bona-fide
estimate can void the extension retroactively under Reg §1.6081-4(a));
§7508A postponements; §6501/§6511 clocks for every year in scope
(`records.md` §9); open examination or notice (`scenarios/audit-response.md`).
Late, unpaid, or an expiring extension ⇒ `DEADLINE_EXPOSURE` (§8) regardless of
workpaper completeness.

**Step 1 — facts.**

1. **Status** determined 12/31, subject to §6013(a)(3), §7703(b), §2(a).
   Dependents with relationship, DOB, support, residency, and SSN-by-due-date
   where a credit requires it. → `credits.md`.
2. **Community property** — MFS or RDP in a community-property state runs through
   **Form 8958**. Commonly missed. → `state-residency.md`.
3. **Residency** — resident states, part-year dates, nonresident source states.
   → `state-residency.md`.
4. **Foreign facts before any income work.** An unfiled foreign information
   return suspends the period on the **entire return** (reasonable cause can limit it to related items — `foreign.md` §4) under §6501(c)(8), so this cannot be
   deferred. → `foreign.md`, `foreign-escalation.md`.
5. **Authority** — load `authority.md`, the target-year rules file, and
   `rules/manifest.json`.
6. **Prior year** — carryforwards, standing elections, prior-year total tax (the
   exact line, per `quarterly.md`), lifetime basis, suspended losses by activity,
   AMT credit. If it cannot be evidenced, do not stop at "provisional": pull the
   Return Transcript and Record of Account, then **Form 4506** where 8606, 8949,
   or 8283 detail matters — transcripts do not show Form 8606.
   → `scenarios/irs-transcripts.md`, `year-over-year.md`.
7. **Permanent records current** — nothing unprocessed in `records/**`
   (`records.md`).
8. **Document completeness** — `pending-docs.md` and the W&I transcript (§2.3).

**Step 2 — engagement posture.** Output is workpapers; the taxpayer bears §6662
and no preparer bears §6694 here — state it in `review.md`. Transcripts require
the taxpayer's IRS account or a signed **2848** (represent) / **8821** (inspect).
**Circular 230 §10.21**: on finding a prior-year error, advise the taxpayer of it,
its consequences, and the correction — it does not authorize correcting it or
notifying the IRS. Produces `PRIOR_YEAR_ERROR_IDENTIFIED` (§8).

### 2.1 Information returns the taxpayer must *file*

Independent penalties, routinely skipped: **Schedule C lines I/J** and the
**Schedule E** equivalent (§6721/§6722, uncapped for intentional disregard under
§6721(e)); **Schedule H** for a household employee; and **nominee reporting**
where a 1099 or K-1 carries the wrong TIN — report the gross on the matching
return, back it out on a nominee line (Reg §1.6041-1(a)), and issue a nominee
1099. That last is the most common structural CP2000 in a multi-entity portfolio.

### 2.2 FBAR and Form 8938 are separate regimes

⚠ Many taxpayers have **both** on the same accounts; satisfying one never
satisfies the other. They differ on threshold, covered assets, filer population,
channel (**BSA E-Filing only** for FBAR, needing FinCEN Form 114a authorization,
vs. a return attachment), deadline mechanics (FBAR: April 15 with an
**automatic** extension to October 15, independent of Form 4868), and penalty.
This is a routing aid, not a sufficient basis for concluding an obligation exists
or does not — both regimes carry exceptions. Conclude from `foreign.md`.

**§6501(c)(8) is a hard hold**: an unfiled **IRC** foreign information return
(8938, 5471, 8865, 8858, 3520, 3520-A) suspends the limitations period on the entire return (a reasonable-cause showing can limit the suspension to the related items — `foreign.md` §4 owns the qualification). **FinCEN 114
is a Title 31 filing and does not trigger it.** Unresolved ⇒ `RETURN_HOLD`,
never `PROVISIONAL`.

### 2.3 Wage & Income transcript reconciliation

⚠ **Timing:** W&I is not complete until roughly May of Y+1 and accretes through
Y+2. Filing before completeness is an **accepted risk recorded in `review.md`**,
not silence. MFJ needs both SSNs; a kiddie-tax case needs the dependent's.

Output is a three-way tie — W&I item → source document → form line — with two
exception lists. ⚠ **The converse invariant is the actual CP2000 preventer: a W&I
item with no return line is a `RETURN_HOLD`.**

## 3. Limitation ordering (control)

Execution order, not background. Mechanics: `loss-limitations.md`.

```
TIER 1 — PER ACTIVITY
  0. Distributions reduce basis BEFORE losses (§705/§733, Reg §1.704-1(d)(2);
     §1367(a)(2) for S corps). Excess is §731/§1368 gain, not a suspended loss.
  1. Basis        computed under §705/§722/§742/§752 (partnership) or §1367
                  (S corp, with debt basis separate). §704(d)/§1366(d) are the
                  LOSS LIMITATIONS keyed to that basis — never cite them as the
                  basis authority.
  2. At-risk      §465 → Form 6198        (§465(e) recapture has a second leg)
  3. Passive      §469 → Form 8582        (PTPs: §469(k), per PTP → ptp.md)

TIER 2 — TAXPAYER LEVEL (aggregate)
  4. Excess business loss §461(l) → Form 461, computed without §172 and §199A
     (§461(l)(3)(A)(i)). Disallowance becomes an NOL — it changes character.
  5. NOL §172 — BY VINTAGE; verify the limitation percentage and CARES carve-outs.

INDEPENDENT, same return: §1211(b)/§1212(b) capital loss; §163(d) investment
interest (carryforward, and the §163(d)(4)(B)(iii) election); §163(j) excess
business interest (per partnership).

THEN, on what survived:
  6. QBI §199A — a suspended loss is not a QBI item until allowed; pre-2018
     disallowed losses never become QBI items. §199A has its OWN carryforward.
  7. AMT §55–59 — separate basis, depreciation, and ISO tracks.
  8. NIIT §1411 (own MAGI) and Additional Medicare — §3101(b)(2) wages,
     §1401(b)(2) SE, §3201(a) RRTA, combined on Form 8959 against a
     filing-status threshold. There is NO MAGI for Additional Medicare.
  9. Credits — nonrefundable in their own ordering, then refundable.
```

## 6. Sale of a residence (§121) — sequence control

The highest-risk cross-schedule path. This section owns the **order and the
gates**; the rental-year mechanics are `scenarios/rental-properties.md`.

1. **Threshold gates** — ownership and use 2-of-5 (§121(a)); **no exclusion if
   gain was excluded on another sale within 2 years (§121(b)(3))**; surviving
   spouse gets the joint maximum if sold within 2 years of death (§121(b)(4));
   ⚠ **a prior §1031 bars §121 for
   5 years (§121(d)(10)) — a threshold disqualifier, so test it first.**
   ⚠ **§121(b)(2) is asymmetric on a joint return:** for the full MFJ exclusion
   **either** spouse may satisfy the **ownership** test, but **both** must satisfy
   the **use** test and **neither** may be barred by §121(b)(3). Fail only the use
   test as to one spouse and the return does not lose the exclusion — it falls to
   the **sum of the separately computed** amounts (§121(b)(2)(B)), which is
   usually the single-filer amount, not zero. Compute it; do not default either
   way.
2. **Nonqualified use (§121(b)(5))** — prorated over the **total period of
   ownership**, not the 5-year window. ⚠ **§121(b)(5)(C)(ii)(I) excepts trailing
   use within the five-year period after the property was last the principal
   residence.** So rental *after* the last residency period generally is not
   nonqualified use, while rental *before* it is. Build the use timeline; do not
   apply a categorical rule in either direction. Also excepted: temporary absence
   ≤2 years, qualified official extended duty, and any period before 2009.
3. **§121(c) partial exclusion** prorates the **maximum exclusion amount**, not
   the gain. ⚠ The §121(b)(1)/(b)(2)(A) maximum itself is an exact value under
   `authority.md` — resolve it for the target year rather than assuming the
   long-standing figures, and note that §121 has **never been indexed**, so a
   "that number hasn't changed" assumption is right for the wrong reason and
   still has to be verified.
4. **§121(d)(6)** — depreciation after 5/6/1997 is never excludable; basis is
   reduced by depreciation **allowed, but not less than allowable**
   (§1016(a)(2)). Where none was claimed for two or more years the remedy is
   **Form 3115 with a §481(a) adjustment**, not an amended return.
5. **Reg §1.165-9(b)(2)** — on a converted residence, basis for **loss** is the
   lesser of FMV at conversion or adjusted basis.
6. **§469(g) release** on a fully taxable disposition — often the largest number
   on the return. → `loss-limitations.md`.
7. **Character** — unrecaptured §1250 capped at the lesser of depreciation
   adjustments or total gain (§1(h)(6)), computed on the Schedule D worksheet;
   §1250 ordinary recapture on non-SL property; business-use portion to
   **Form 4797**; then NIIT.
8. **§121(f)** permits **electing out** where preserving §1031 is worth more.
   Rev. Proc. 2005-14 governs the concurrent case.
9. **Reflect the 1099-S** even where gain is fully excluded.
10. **State** — check the resident state's own regime. → `state-residency.md`.

## 7. Completeness invariants

Arithmetic identities are owned by `estimate.md` and asserted **by reference**.
Owned here:

- Digital-asset question answered explicitly (disposition, payment, or reward =
  Yes; holding alone = No).
- **Schedule B Part III** foreign questions answered from `foreign.md`, never
  from the absence of a 1099.
- Payments tie to source documents, not memory.
- **Form 2210** computed, waived, or annualized; §6654 tested **by installment
  period**. → `withholding-penalties.md`.
- §2.3 converse invariant: a W&I item with no return line is a hold.
- Outbound information returns (§2.1) addressed.
- §6501(c)(8): no unfiled IRC foreign information return.
- Elections disclosed where required — §469 grouping (Rev. Proc. 2010-13),
  §199A aggregation, Reg §1.469-9(g).
- **Form 7203** attached for any S-corp loss, distribution, disposition, or loan
  repayment — a **1040** attachment the entity return does not carry.
- Schedule E page 2 "not at risk" box consistent with Form 6198.
- State withholding footed to source documents.

**Penalty and disclosure posture, decided before filing.** Confidence ladder:
reasonable basis **plus disclosure**; substantial authority undisclosed
(Reg §1.6662-4(d)(3)); more likely than not. §6662(d) substantial understatement;
40% tiers under §6662(b)(7)/(j) and §6662(h). **Form 8275/8275-R** attaches to the
original timely-filed (or superseding) return, or to a **qualified amended
return** — whose availability requires testing all four Reg §1.6664-2(c)(3)
cutoff events, not merely "before contact" (→ `notices-amendments.md` §2).
Disclosure cures neither negligence nor valuation misstatement.

**Filing mechanics.** Superseding vs. qualified amended vs. 1040-X, and
§301.9100 relief for a missed election, are owned by `notices-amendments.md` §2.
Attachments: Form 8283 Section B needs appraiser and donee signatures and travels
either as a **PDF attachment** to the e-filed return or by mail with **Form 8453**
(the 8453 route also carries 1098-C, 2848, 3115, 8332) — `itemized.md` §5.2 owns
the rule. **§7502** timely-mailing is
often the only surviving proof of filing.

## 8. Output and completion contract

Under `individual/FY<YYYY>/`: `annual/workpapers/wp-<topic>.md`;
`review.md` (from `templates/individual-review.md.template`); `return-package.md`
(from `templates/individual-return-package.md.template`). Year-crossing values
roll into `carryforwards.json` **only** when the user marks the year filed.

| Status | Meaning | Maps to `close-estimate.md` |
|---|---|---|
| `RETURN_HOLD` | An invariant fails, a load-bearing input is `UNREADABLE`/`NOT_PRESENT`, an authority hold is open, or §6501(c)(8) exposure is unresolved | ⊇ `{ESTIMATE_HOLD, AUTHORITY_HOLD}` |
| `DEADLINE_EXPOSURE` | Late, unpaid, or an expiring extension | orthogonal |
| `PROVISIONAL` | Complete but dependent on **projected or unfiled inputs**. ⚠ An unverified rules parameter is **not** provisional — under `authority.md` it is a hold | `PROVISIONAL` |
| `DRAFT_VERIFIED_INPUTS` | Inputs evidenced and verified; not yet reviewed | same |
| `READY_FOR_PRACTITIONER_REVIEW` | All invariants pass, authority recorded, holds cleared | same |
| `PRIOR_YEAR_ERROR_IDENTIFIED` | Circular 230 §10.21 advice obligation triggered; §6511 tested | orthogonal |

A first-year filer with no prior year is not permanently `PROVISIONAL` — record
the absence as a fact and use the current-year safe harbor.

Never present a held or unknown line as zero. Never label a package ready while a
§2 row with facts is unresolved.

If the `SKILL.md` session disclaimer has not been shown — a user can enter
directly through `individual/README.md` — show it now.

End every individual engagement with: **"Verify with a licensed practitioner
before filing."**
