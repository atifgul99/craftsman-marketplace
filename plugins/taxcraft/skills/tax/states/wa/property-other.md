# WA Personal Property Tax & Unclaimed Property

## Personal Property Tax

### What it is
County-level tax on business personal property (equipment, computers, furniture, machinery) owned or on hand as of **January 1** each year. Filed with the **county assessor**, not DOR.

Report to the assessor for the county where the business property is physically located (e.g. King County → kingcounty.gov/assessor). Determine the county from the entity's physical work location in `entities/<slug>/entity.md`, not its mailing address.

### What's taxable
- Computers, monitors, servers, printers
- Office furniture
- Any equipment used in the business
- Does **not** include: inventory held for sale, licensed vehicles (taxed separately), intangibles (IP, securities)

### Due date
**April 30** each year. File the Personal Property Tax Listing Form with King County Assessor.

### How to file
1. King County Assessor e-file portal: assessor.kingcounty.gov
2. Report assets by category with original cost and year acquired
3. County applies depreciation schedule → assessed value → levy rate = tax bill
4. Tax bill mailed ~Aug/Sep; due in two halves (Apr 30 + Oct 31 of following year)

### Assets to report (as of Jan 1 of the assessment year)
- All business equipment: computers, monitors, furniture, tools — original cost and year acquired
- Assets fully §179-expensed or bonus-depreciated for income tax are **still reportable**
- Build the list from the entity's fixed-asset schedule / depreciation workpaper

### Note on §179
Federal §179 expensing reduces **income tax** basis to zero but does **not** exempt the asset from personal property tax. The county uses its own depreciation table regardless of tax treatment.

## Unclaimed Property (WA)

### What it is
Monetary assets or tangible property held by a business that has had no owner contact for **1 year or more**. Must be reported and remitted to DOR.

### Common items
- Uncashed payroll or vendor checks
- Customer overpayments / credits
- Security deposits
- Gift certificates (after dormancy period)

### Due date
**November 1** each year (for property dormant as of June 30)

### Where to report
DOR Unclaimed Property portal: **ucp.dor.wa.gov**

### Applicability
Risk scales with payroll and vendor count. Monitor any uncashed checks to contractors, refunds, or credit balances outstanding > 1 year.
